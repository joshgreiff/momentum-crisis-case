  - **Our people:** there's no business without our employees and drivers. We hold ourselves accountable, but we fix the system instead of scapegoating individuals.
  - **Recovery:** structural changes, including to leadership, which I'll come back to. (Save the resignation for the close.)
- **Decision timeline (5):** "Within hours we grounded the fleet, and within 48 hours we publicly disclosed the cause, including that we'd known for a month."
- **Our crisis command center (no slide):** "I built a crisis command center every executive could access. It kept every email and update we received in order, plus every decision we made in response, so we could lay out the facts, catch conflicting messages and stay coordinated. ## Griffin (CFO): slides 12–13

Lead with how we survive.
- **How we survive:** we earn about $8.1B a year on $75.8B revenue, so we can absorb settlements and the cost of human-driven deliveries. The risk is short-term cash: $2.1B against $19.0B long-term debt and $758M a year in interest. Protect it: confirm the credit line, pause nonessential spending and new autonomous investment, lean on insurance.
- **Legal exposure:** wrongful death and injury claims, punitive damages because of the month of prior knowledge, federal fines, shareholder suits. The $15B Globex deal and about $700M fleet investment are at risk if the 2018 law is rolled back.
- **Mitigation:** notify insurers, reserve for claims, fair early settlements, keep Amazon and major customers.
