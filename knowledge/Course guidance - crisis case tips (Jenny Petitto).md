# Tips for the Crisis Case (from Jenny Petitto, recitation leader)

- Emails will be initiated four times during recitation and at various times over the next several days, including evenings and weekends. Teams should respond to the issue and to the FCOB inbox within 24 hours.
- First questions as a team: what are the problems, what are the underlying causes, and what can we do about them? Take the initiative now; do not wait to be told what to do.
- Decisions must sometimes be made without all the facts. Good and bad decisions are easily made in the first 24 hours.
- Establish structure and timeliness for the team's work and for communicating with stakeholders, employees and the public. Identify the stakeholders and make sure employees, stakeholders and the public receive the necessary information. Provide updates regularly and as more information becomes available.
- Public perception is very important (e.g., Tylenol 1982).
- Copy teammates individually and the FCOB emails as appropriate so all are informed. Resend emails that do not get through.
- Evaluation areas, at a minimum: Teamwork and Decision Making, Effective Communication, and Time Management. The assignment is intentionally ambiguous to simulate a real scenario.
- Board presentations: Friday, November 6. 15 minutes total per team, including time for Board questions; the team distributes presentation time among members. The Board will interrupt with questions ("why did you do this?", defend your decisions) to test how decisions hold up. Delivery is assessed (eye contact, transitions, volume).
- In presentations: do not repeat a good point someone already made; use data and figures to support the team's case; do not summarize the case, because the Board already knows it.
